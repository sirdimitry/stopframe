Third-party components

StopFrame bundles dynamically loaded Qt/PySide6 libraries. The corresponding
Qt 6.11.2 source is available at https://download.qt.io/archive/qt/6.11/6.11.2/
and PySide6 source at https://code.qt.io/cgit/pyside/pyside-setup.git/.
Qt license texts in this folder come from the v6.11.2 qtbase LICENSES directory:
https://github.com/qt/qtbase/tree/v6.11.2/LICENSES

Other Python distributions retain their package metadata and supplied license
files under Contents/Resources/*.dist-info. These notices do not replace those
upstream licenses. Qt and the other bundled libraries remain the work of their
respective contributors.
